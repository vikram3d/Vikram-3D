# Form & Motion — design direction

Preserve Vikram's black (#080808), ivory (#ebe9e2), and cyan (#83e8f9) identity. Michroma display headings remain bold, while longer copy stays readable in Arial and Coda. The existing animated shard field remains the hero asset; no new imagery is introduced.

Sequence: viewport-aware hero and transparent glass biography → enlarged short About → three scrolling project cards → compact, complete contact footer. The dedicated Work page retains its seven-cartridge console. Playground is removed.

The framed navigation becomes a floating glass bar on scroll, keeping page links accessible. Existing GSAP/ScrollTrigger entrance and card motions are retained. The reduced-motion setting disables transitions and pinning. No additional scrolling engine or 3D scene is introduced. User-supplied films and the previously documented asset sources remain unchanged.
