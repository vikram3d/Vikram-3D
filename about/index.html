ABOUT / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELSVIKRAM R

Curious by nature.
Creative by choice.

I’m Vikram R, a 3D artist and motion designer exploring the space between imagination and experience.

I work primarily with Blender, bringing together modelling, lighting, texturing, animation, and rendering to create cinematic visuals and engaging digital experiences.

With one year of experience as a 3D artist at Arijentek, I create 3D visuals, service-based animations, and creative content for websites and social media.

My goal is simple: transform ideas into visually stunning experiences through form, light, and motion.
